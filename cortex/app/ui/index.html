Skills you taught

How it works

  1. Cortex records the screen 10× a second, together with the keys and mouse you use.
  2. While playing, it finds the moment in your recording that looks most like the screen now and does what you did next. This takes about 50 ms.
  3. With a Gemini key, Gemini decides which skill to use and when, and fills the gaps with its own moves.
  4. Record the same name again to add more examples. Variety helps a lot.

It only records while the game is in front, and never records ⌘ shortcuts.

Settings

Keys are stored in your Mac's Keychain.

Gemini (free AI)

Free at Google AI Studio: sign in, click “Create API key”, paste it here. It takes about a minute and needs no card.

The free tier allows about 15 per minute. More requests mean faster corrections. Between requests, Cortex keeps playing.
Claude (optional, paid)

Only used if there's no Gemini key.

Playing

Welcome to Cortex

Cortex plays games on your Mac for you. Tell it what to do, or teach it by playing. First it needs permission to see the game and press keys.

Free AI brain (optional)

With a free Google Gemini key, Cortex understands any game and any instruction. Without one, it can still copy what you teach it.

You're ready

  1. Open your game and pick it on the Play page.
  2. Type what it should do, then press Start.
  3. Switch to the game. Cortex starts after a 3-second countdown.
  4. Press F12 to stop or F11 to pause, any time.

Only use Cortex in single-player or offline games.
